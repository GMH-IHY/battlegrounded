# Data Entry: Region One — Diomedes

Located at Emerald Terrace. Lore only: it doesn't set any gameplay rule.

## Origins in the Aphrodite Era

One of the eight core regions of the land. This terrace had an interesting position, as it was unique in the Aphrodite Era in that it had a keystone species that dwelled only in this terrace. The Lilliputians were small semi-sentient beings and played an important role in the world ecosystem as they exercised near absolute dominance over this region. Small cities spread for miles and miles underground, the insects that lived here were all tamed and used as vehicles to transport them, and being small they avoided the predators from above.

Ancient Lilliputian text alludes to an unknown origin of a giant structure that looked almost like the Lilliputians. No one was sure what these Lilliputians were, but it would seem that they were not meant to be here; lifestyle diaries state that they came from a different plane and escaped to flourish in a world that they could live comfortably. Lilliputians thought the structure was given to them as a gift; it seemed to have an inherent fondness for them. The only threat to the Lilliputians were large bugs that they could not tame, and the structure got rid of the pests.

Lilliputians and the structure had what seemed to be a symbiotic relationship. They called the structure Aeneas.

## Changes in the Apollo Era

Old news clippings noted the land turned dark upon the arrival of Aeneas. Also the area saw no large-sized animals, and the bugs the Lilliputians had domesticated developed a fungus on their bodies which caused them to hourly become exponentially larger than usual; which could indicate that both the Lilliputians and Aeneas may be resistant to the many unspecified and unknown plagues of this world. Because it seems clear that the Lotus is one — of the first — plagues introduced upon the land.

Eventually, during the Apollo Era, the celestial body Zeus delivered a large beam of light to Aeneas which destroyed the construct and made the Lilliputians extremely vulnerable to their immediate environment, causing the death of the entire population, and as it exists today, only a handful of cities remain in this area.

## Current World Relevance

It appears that Primary Zeus must have played with Aeneas, which is why Aeneas is wandering around like a corpse. It will attack anything that is not like itself and to core when someone is crying out to help the Lilliputians. We were only able to communicate to the cities that are hidden from the surface, who share that most Lilliputians now live underground. They are completely unaware of Aeneas happening. The information can be corroborated by the fact that soldiers' bodies who tried to kill Aeneas have been eaten inside out by the Lilliputians. This is a suggestion that the Lilliputians are small enough to enter through the seams of modern armor.

We know that Lotus occurs in the form of fungus and spores and can be an exceedingly hazardous aerobic plague when it enters, spreads through and replaces living tissue.
