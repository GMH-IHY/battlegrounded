# Data Entry: Magic System — Lotus Fungus

Magic type: Scourge Magic [Howl]
Human variant: Moth [Weakness: Fire] [Resistant: Shadow]

Lore only: it doesn't set any gameplay rule. How Lotus Fungus plays is in [../DesignPlan.md](../DesignPlan.md) and [../MoveData.md](../MoveData.md).

Lotus' practical use in regard to being used for your own benefit has not been lost. Attaching Lotus to your body is a traumatic process and can be hard to maintain, as Lotus is constantly increasing your agency at the expense of slowly consuming your body; knowing when to stop and when to go is VERY IMPORTANT. Lotus, in addition, behaves based on stimulus: when you are healing, Lotus recesses, and when in pain it grows substantially. Because Lotus grows so fast, it is almost as if it is evolving... which is why some people have been able to use this growth to make weapons out of Lotus or make armor out of Lotus. But this is a dangerous practice.
